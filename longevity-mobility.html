Maud's Toolkit

Habits, techniques, and the stuff that costs nothing but doing it.

Dead hang, 20-30 seconds, twice a week

A doorway pull-up bar is enough. Grip strength is one of the strongest predictors of longevity we have — and it's the first thing to go if you never train it.

Grip strength

The sitting-rising test, quarterly

Sit down on the floor and stand back up, no hands, no knees. Score yourself out of 10. It sounds silly until you realize it's genuinely correlated with how long you'll live.

Quarterly check

Carry something heavy, regularly

Groceries in both hands, all the way to the kitchen, no setting them down halfway. Loaded carries build the strength you actually use in real life.

Daily habit

Balance on one foot while you brush your teeth

Two minutes, twice a day, costs nothing. Balance is the quiet skill that prevents the fall that changes everything.
